# Lattely

Lattely helps people in Barcelona find specialty coffee shops by location and compare them using ratings.

## Language

**Rating**:
A score from 1 to 5 that a signed-in person submits through Lattely to express their assessment of a coffee shop. Each person has one current rating per shop and can update it.

**Coffee shop rating**:
The average score submitted by people using Lattely for a coffee shop. Show it alongside the rating count and use it to order results.
_Avoid_: Most rated (can mean either the highest score or the most ratings)

**Rating count**:
The number of individual ratings people have submitted through Lattely for a coffee shop.

**Specialty coffee shop**:
A coffee shop where carefully sourced coffee and how it is prepared are a central focus.

**Search radius**:
The distance from the selected location within which coffee shops appear in results. The default is 2 km; people can choose 500 m, 1 km, 2 km, 5 km, 10 km, or Anywhere (all listed specialty coffee shops across Barcelona).
